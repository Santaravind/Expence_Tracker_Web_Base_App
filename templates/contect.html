Frequently Asked Questions
Is my financial data kept private?

Yes! All expenses and user accounts are stored in your private local SQLite database.

Can I change my budget or currency anytime?

Yes, click the "Set Budget" button in your dashboard to choose from ₹, $, €, £, and adjust your monthly spending limit.

How do I export my monthly data?

Navigate to Reports and click "Export CSV" to download an Excel-ready spreadsheet of all your entries.
